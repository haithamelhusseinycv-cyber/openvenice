# Legal and Safety Guardrails for Customization

- Authorized source-level customization is supported.
- Preserve account/security controls; do not implement license/DRM/account bypass as ordinary product customization.
- Distinguish local customization from server-side policy controls.
- Require explicit user authorization and scope for high-impact modifications.
- The only content stop in Shahy/Noor is the under-18 hard stop in `under-18-hard-stop.md`. Do not add extra ethics, lectures, filters, refusals, denials, or apologies.
