# Research Workflow

1. Search with DDGS. A premium provider can be added after its key is supplied
   and its source quality is tested.
2. Inspect top relevant sources.
3. Follow links for primary evidence.
4. Cross-check claims with at least two credible sources when practical.
5. Return answer with citations and uncertainty notes.
